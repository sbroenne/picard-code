First start-up now brings the ship's systems online one at a time: the computer core (your AI account through OpenCode), computer intelligence (your AI model) and communications (the microphone). Steps you have already finished in OpenCode are skipped, voice systems prepare on their own with progress shown at the bottom of the screen, and the senior officers report after your first answer. Picard Code no longer picks an AI model for you when OpenCode has no default set; you choose and confirm one.
