# Community code of conduct

Picard Code welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Treat contributors, users, and maintainers with respect. This applies to issues,
pull requests, Discussions, and other spaces representing the project.

## Working together

- Be patient, considerate, and clear. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and constructive feedback. Accept corrections gracefully.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and other people's creative work.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for different experiences.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For non-sensitive project conduct concerns, contact the maintainer,
[@sbroenne](https://github.com/sbroenne), in the affected GitHub thread.
Do not post private information to make a report. For harassment, threats, or
sensitive abuse, use [GitHub's private abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
The project does not offer a separate private messaging inbox.

The maintainer may edit or remove disruptive content, close discussions, or
restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate: clarification or a
warning for minor problems, and temporary or permanent exclusion for serious
or repeated harm. Reports are considered as availability permits; no response
deadline is promised.

This policy follows the welcoming principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and uses original
wording for this project's reporting arrangements.
