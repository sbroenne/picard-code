A tribute to Sir Patrick Stewart and Captain Jean-Luc Picard. Unofficial, made with deep respect and gratitude.

It runs on this computer and does its coding through OpenCode, with the model provider you choose.

The Tribute switch in the left navigation controls occasional sourced facts, gentle humour about the Captain, and offers of Picard or Patrick Stewart stories. Picard stories describe fictional adventures; Stewart stories describe the actor's career. Turning it off keeps Picard's identity and stories you explicitly request.

Automatic facts appear on screen with a quiet tricorder sound after the answer finishes. Requested answers and story invitations are still spoken.

The Bridge crew switch in the left navigation brings the six bridge officers on or off duty. When it is off, the computer answers every request itself.

Facts may include story spoilers. This is a growing, reviewed collection, not a complete biography. Requested Picard and Patrick Stewart questions use Wikipedia, with source links on screen. Automatic facts and stories use reviewed summaries and make no web requests.
